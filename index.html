Yanyan Zou

Email / Google Scholar / LinkedIn / X / GitHub

I am a research scientist at JD Future Academy under the TGT programme, working with Jiachen Liu and Nan Duan. My research centers on multimodal large language models: post-training, agents, and multi-modal data synthesis.

Before this, I spent six years at JD Retail building industrial-scale generative recommendation and NLP systems, with publications at SIGIR, RecSys, AAAI, and EMNLP along the way.

Feel free to reach out for discussion and potential collaborations.
